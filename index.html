My Projects

Two-Stage Reducer Gearbox Siemens NX

Two-stage reducer gearbox built in Siemens NX during my summer internship at the Siemens Centre of Excellence, MIT Campus, Anna University (CAD and CAE). It follows an existing reference design and is not an original design of mine. I modeled the casing, gears, retaining rings, bearings and output shaft as individual parts (sketch, extrude, revolve, edge blend) and assembled them using concentric, hinge and gear constraints.
